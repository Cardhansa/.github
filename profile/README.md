# Cardhansa

**Focus on the game. We will handle the rest.**

Cardhansa is the most convenient way to buy and sell cards in Europe. Find singles in our convenient search or import your list from ManaBox, Moxfield or Archidekt. Ship with no label to print and get your money in as little as 2 days after you ship.

Right now we buy and sell Magic: The Gathering cards. Players decide which game we add next.
